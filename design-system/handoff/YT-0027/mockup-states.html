Two static states that the interactive mockup doesn't show frame-by-frame. Read alongside decision-log.md Q4 (scrubber) and Q6 (queue). All other states (play/pause, queue collapsed inline, background variants, haptics) live in mockup.html.

A · Scrubber being dragged

Q4 · scrubber
Mid-drag state at ~36% through the track. Track expands 4→8 pt, thumb grows 12→18 pt, time labels jump from secondary 12 pt to primary 14 pt and gain weight. Artwork shrinks to 0.92 (subordinate to the 0.85 pause-scale). Haptic `.selection` ticks fire every 10 s of scrub distance — never per frame.
