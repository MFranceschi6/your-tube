Custom UTI. The Files-app surfacing fix (YT-0047) needs UTExportedTypeDeclarations for com.yourtube.playlist conforming to public.json, AND CFBundleDocumentTypes declaring YourTube as Owner of that UTI. See decision-log § 1 and swiftui-spec § Custom UTType.
AirDrop receive. Both .onOpenURL (warm-launch) AND scene(_:openURLContexts:) (cold-launch) wire to the same ImportRouter. Missing either is a YT-0047-class regression. See decision-log § 8.
Privacy. Exported file contains only schemaVersion, id, name, createdAt, updatedAt, and a tracks array. No idfv, no idfa, no keychain, no auth tokens. JSONEncoder uses [.prettyPrinted, .sortedKeys] for byte-deterministic output (parity-fixture contract).
Cite, do not redefine. Format is owned by docs/api-contracts.md. Toast copy lives in toast-catalog/copy.md. The op overlay is state-catalog C15; the inline error styling is C16.
